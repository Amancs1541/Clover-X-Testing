Everything in one place

Conversation that can go somewhere.

Start with a message. Move to a call. Find a service nearby. Gabber keeps everyday actions close to the conversation.

  1. 01

    Chat naturally

    Personal conversations, group chats, voice notes, media and status updates in an interface that feels immediately familiar.

    • 1:1 & group chats
    • Voice notes
    • Media
    • Status updates
  2. 02

    Talk face to face, and draw together

    Move from messages to voice or video calls, including group calls. Draw together turns a call into a shared canvas: sketch, highlight and doodle live on everyone's screen.

    • Voice calls
    • Video calls
    • Group calls
    • Draw together
  3. 03

    Discover local

    Explore nearby restaurants, shops, hotels and services by category and distance, see ratings and verification, then start a chat with the business directly inside Gabber.

    • Categories
    • 5–50 km radius
    • Ratings
    • Verified badges

Gabber for Business

Let nearby customers find you first.

Create a business presence, show what you offer, handle enquiries and keep customer conversations, orders and payments together.

  1. 01

    Be discoverable

    Appear when people explore businesses around them: restaurants, hotels, food stalls, shops and services.

  2. 02

    Build confidence

    Submit business details, location and an optional GST number for verification, and earn the verified badge.

  3. 03

    Serve in chat

    Turn discovery into conversation: share orders in the chat and let customers pay securely without leaving Gabber.
